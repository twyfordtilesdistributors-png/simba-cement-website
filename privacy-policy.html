SIMBA CEMENT

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through the Simba Cement website may be handled when you contact us, request an order, submit customer feedback or otherwise communicate with our sales team.

1. Information We May Receive

Depending on how you use the website, we may receive your name, telephone number, delivery location, order details, notes or customer feedback. You may choose not to provide optional information such as your name on the feedback form.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to respond to enquiries, process and confirm orders, arrange delivery, provide customer service, respond to feedback and communicate about a request you have made.

3. WhatsApp Communications

Orders and feedback from this website may open WhatsApp using the business number displayed on the website. Information you send through WhatsApp is also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices.

4. Payments

The website does not collect card or mobile-money payment credentials directly. Any payment arrangements are confirmed separately with the sales team.

5. Data Sharing

We do not publish your personal information on the website. Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to fulfil an order, arrange delivery, comply with a legal requirement or protect the business and its customers.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, order records, accounting, dispute handling or legal obligations.

7. Your Choices

You may contact us to ask questions about information you have submitted or to request correction of inaccurate details. Some records may need to be retained where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Simba Cement through WhatsApp on 0756 311 102.

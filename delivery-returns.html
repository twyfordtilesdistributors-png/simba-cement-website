SIMBA CEMENT

DELIVERY & RETURNS

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This policy explains how orders, delivery and returns are handled when products are requested through the Simba Cement website.

1. Order Confirmation

Website orders are sent to the sales team through WhatsApp for confirmation. The team will confirm product availability, quantities, current prices and delivery arrangements before the order is treated as accepted.

2. Delivery

Delivery availability and timing depend on the destination, order size, stock and transport arrangements. Delivery charges, where applicable, are agreed with the customer before dispatch.

3. Customer Responsibility

Please provide a complete delivery location and reachable telephone number. The customer should ensure that the agreed delivery point is accessible to the delivery vehicle and that someone is available to receive the order.

4. Inspection on Delivery

Customers should inspect products as soon as practical after delivery and raise visible shortages, damage or incorrect items with the sales team promptly.

5. Returns

Returns or replacements may be considered for products that are materially defective, damaged before delivery, or supplied incorrectly, subject to inspection and confirmation by the sales team. Products that have been used, altered, stored improperly or damaged after delivery may not qualify for return.

6. Incorrect Orders

Where an order is changed or cancelled after confirmation, any resulting transport, handling or other costs will be discussed with the customer before the change is processed.

7. Cement and Construction Materials

Because cement, steel and related materials are project-specific construction inputs, customers should confirm quantities, grades, sizes and intended applications before placing an order.

8. Contact for Delivery & Returns

Contact the sales team on WhatsApp on 0756 311 102 with your order details and the reason for the request.
